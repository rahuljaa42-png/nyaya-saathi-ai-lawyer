### Where you stand and what to do
1. **Collect your proof.** Gather the items in your checklist below, especially proof of payment and the handover date.
2. **Send a written demand.** Send the demand letter below by WhatsApp and email, and by registered post if you can. Ask for the refund within 15 days and for a written list of any deductions.
3. **Check your state's rent law.** Deposit rules depend on your state.
    - Rent is a state subject, so the rules on security deposits depend on your state. The Model Tenancy Act, 2021 is a central model law; it applies only where your state has adopted it. [R-STATE]
    - Under the Model Tenancy Act, a residential security deposit can be at most two months' rent, and the landlord must refund it when you hand back vacant possession, after deducting what you genuinely owe.
