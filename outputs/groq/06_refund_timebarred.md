## Input

In 12 March 2024 I bought a laptop online for Rs 54,000. It was defective and the seller never refunded me. Can I still complain?

## Output

> ⚠️ You are close to or past the 2-year limit for consumer complaints. Act now and get legal advice.

**What happened**
You bought a laptop online on 12 Mar 2024 for ₹54,000. It turned out to be defective and the seller has not refunded you.

**Where you stand**
- The value of your claim (₹54,000) is below ₹50 lakh, so the appropriate forum is the District Consumer Commission [CP-FORUM].
- A consumer complaint must be filed within two years of the cause of action [CP-LIMIT]. Your purchase was on 12 Mar 2024, so you are still within the time limit.

**What to do next**
1. **Try a free grievance first** – call the National Consumer Helpline (1915 or 1800‑11‑4000) or use consumerhelpline.gov.in [CP-NCH].
2. If the seller does not resolve the issue, file a complaint online on the e‑Jagriti portal [CP-EJAGRITI].

**What to collect**
- Order ID and invoice (or receipt) [Evidence to collect]
- Proof of payment (bank statement, UPI screenshot)
- All chat/email/WhatsApp messages with the seller about the defect or refund request
- Screenshots of the return/refund status page
- Any warranty or return‑policy information shown at purchase

**When to get a real lawyer**
- If the seller refuses to cooperate after the helpline and e‑Jagriti filing.
- If you need help drafting a legal notice or representing you at the Consumer Commission.
- You can obtain free legal aid through NALSA [LA-NALSA].

*This is general information, not legal advice.*

### When you need a real lawyer
- The other side replies through a lawyer, sends you a notice, or threatens you.
- You are near a time limit, or the amount is large for you.
- You are not sure the facts above are right. This is general information, not legal advice.
Free legal aid: call the NALSA helpline 15100 or visit your District Legal Services Authority. Women and children are eligible whatever they earn; others qualify under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, for example by income. [LA-NALSA]
