## Input

Meri company ne pichhle 2 mahine se salary nahi di. Main abhi bhi kaam kar raha hoon. Har mahine 18000 milta hai. HR bolta hai funds nahi hai.

## Output

**What happened**
- You have been working for the company and have not received salary for the last 2 months (₹18,000 per month). HR says there are no funds.

**Where you stand**
- Under the Code on Wages, 2019, wages for monthly‑paid employees must be paid **before the end of the 7th day of the next month** [W-DUE].
- Since the salary for two months is overdue, the company is already in violation of this provision.

**What to do next**
1. **Raise a written grievance** with HR/management asking for payment and a written explanation. Keep a copy for your records.
2. If no satisfactory response within a reasonable time (e.g., 7 days), you can **file a wage claim** with the authority appointed under the Code (an officer not below Gazetted rank) [W-CLAIM].
3. You may also approach a registered trade union or the Inspector‑cum‑Facilitator to file the claim on your behalf.

**What to collect**
- Offer/appointment letter or any employment contract.
- Salary slips or any pay‑statement showing the amount due.
- Bank statements showing the last salary received.
- All emails / messages you have sent to HR asking for salary.
- Any written response from HR (even if it just says “no funds”).

**When to get a real lawyer**
- If the claim authority does not act or the company refuses to pay even after the claim, consider consulting a labour lawyer.
- If you face any retaliation, threats, or if the amount increases, seek legal counsel immediately.

*This is general information, not legal advice.*
