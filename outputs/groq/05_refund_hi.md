## Input

मैंने अमेज़न से 8,999 रुपये का मोबाइल ऑर्डर किया था, ख़राब निकला। रिटर्न कर दिया पर एक महीने से रिफ़ंड नहीं आया। क्या करूँ?

## Output

**What happened**
- You bought a mobile for ₹8,999 from Amazon, received a defective unit, returned it and have been waiting for a refund for over a month.

**Where you stand**
- Under the Consumer Protection Act, Amazon (an e‑commerce platform) must have a Grievance Officer and must resolve complaints within one month. The delay breaches this duty. [CP-ECOM]
- Your claim is for ₹8,999, which is below ₹5 lakh, so there is no filing fee at the District Consumer Commission. [CP-FEE]
- The dispute value is ₹8,999, so the appropriate forum is the District Consumer Commission. [CP-FORUM]

**What to do next**
1. **Raise a grievance on Amazon** – use the Grievance Officer details (usually in *Help → Contact Us*). Mention the order ID, that the return was confirmed, and ask for the refund within the statutory 48‑hour acknowledgment and one‑month resolution. [CP-ECOM]
2. **If Amazon does not resolve within 30 days**, register a free complaint with the National Consumer Helpline (1915 / 1800‑11‑4000 or consumerhelpline.gov.in). [CP-NCH]
3. **If still unresolved**, file a consumer complaint online on the e‑Jagriti portal (e‑jagriti.gov.in). [CP-EJAGRITI]

**What to collect**
- Order receipt & invoice (you have).
- Return confirmation email/SMS.
- Proof of payment (bank statement or screenshot).
- Screenshots of the return/refund status on Amazon.
- Any chat/email with Amazon customer care (including timestamps).
- Tracking number or proof of delivery of the returned package (if available).

**When to get a real lawyer**
- If Amazon refuses to refund even after the grievance and NCH steps, or if you receive a legal notice, consider consulting a lawyer.
- You can also approach NALSA for free legal aid (call 15100). [LA-NALSA]

*This is general information, not legal advice.*

### कब असली वकील चाहिए

- समय-सीमा पास हो, या रक़म आपके लिए बड़ी हो।
