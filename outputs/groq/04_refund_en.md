## Input

I ordered a washing machine on Flipkart for Rs 23,490 on 2 October 2026. It arrived damaged, I returned it on 6 October and the refund status still says processing. Customer care keeps giving new dates. I have the order ID, invoice and screenshots.

## Output

**What happened**
You bought a washing machine on Flipkart for ₹23,490 on 2 Oct 2026. It arrived damaged, you returned it on 6 Oct 2026 and the refund status still shows *processing* despite multiple promises from customer care.

**Where you stand**
- The transaction value is ₹23,490, which is well below the ₹50 lakh limit for the District Consumer Commission. [CP-FORUM]
- You have a cause of action (the undelivered refund) that arose on 6 Oct 2026, so you are within the two‑year filing window. [CP-LIMIT]
- The e‑commerce platform must have a Grievance Officer and must resolve complaints within one month. [CP-ECOM]

**What to do next**
1. **Raise a free grievance** with the National Consumer Helpline (1915 or 1800‑11‑4000, or consumerhelpline.gov.in). [CP-NCH]
2. If the issue is not resolved within 30 days of your complaint to the Grievance Officer, **file a consumer complaint** in the District Consumer Commission (no filing fee for claims up to ₹5 lakh). [CP-FEE]
3. File the complaint **online** via the e‑Jagriti portal (e‑jagriti.gov.in). [CP-EJAGRITI]

**What to collect**
- Order ID and invoice (you have).
- Screenshots of the refund‑status page and any chat/email with customer care (you have).
- Proof of payment (bank statement or payment receipt).
- Courier receipt or shipping proof for the returned item (if you can obtain it).
- Any email/SMS confirming the return or refund processing.

**When to get a real lawyer**
If the District Commission does not grant a refund or you face a complicated dispute (e.g., the platform refuses to acknowledge the grievance officer), consider contacting a lawyer or legal aid centre. Free legal aid is available through NALSA (call 15100). [LA-NALSA]

*This is general information, not legal advice.*

### When you need a real lawyer
- The other side replies through a lawyer, sends you a notice, or threatens you.
- You are near a time limit, or the amount is large for you.
- You are not sure the facts above are right. This is general information, not legal advice.
Free legal aid: call the NALSA helpline 15100 or visit your District Legal Services Authority. Women and children are eligible whatever they earn; others qualify under Section 12 of the Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987, for example by income. [LA-NALSA]
