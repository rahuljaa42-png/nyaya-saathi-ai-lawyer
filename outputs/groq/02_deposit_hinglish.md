## Input

Bhai mera PG owner mera 25000 deposit wapas nahi kar raha. Maine 1 August 2026 ko room khali kiya tha, chabi bhi de di thi. Ab phone bhi nahi utha raha. Kya karu?

## Output

**What happened**
- You vacated the PG on 1 August 2026, handed over the keys and the owner has not returned your ₹25,000 security deposit.

**Where you stand**
- Under the Model Tenancy Act, 2021 a residential security deposit can be at most two months’ rent and must be refunded when you give vacant possession, after deducting any genuine dues. The landlord must also pay interest for the delay. [**R-MTA**]
- If your state has adopted this model law, the owner is legally obliged to return the deposit (plus interest) now.

**What to do next**
1. Send a **formal written notice** (SMS, WhatsApp, email or registered post) to the PG owner asking for the deposit return within a reasonable time (e.g., 7‑10 days) and mention that interest will accrue under the Model Tenancy Act. Keep a copy.
2. If there is no response, you can **file a civil suit** for recovery of the deposit. The limitation period is roughly three years from the date of default, so you are well within it. [**R-SUIT**]

**What to collect**
- Rent agreement or any document that mentions the security deposit amount and terms.
- Proof of payment (receipt, bank statement, UPI transaction screenshot).
- Photographs or video of the flat when you left, showing its condition.
- All messages (SMS, WhatsApp, email) with the landlord about the deposit and hand‑over of keys.
- A copy of the written notice you send.

**When to get a real lawyer**
- If the owner still ignores your notice.
- If you need help drafting the legal notice or filing the suit.
- If you are unsure whether your state has adopted the Model Tenancy Act.

**Free legal help**
- Call NALSA helpline **15100** or visit your District Legal Services Authority for free assistance. [**LA-NALSA**]

*This is general information, not legal advice.*
